Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 16, 2026

At BeakBuddy, we take your privacy—and the safety of your feathered companions—extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Information: We obtain your email address and profile name via Google, Apple, or Email authentication to manage your account secure access.
  • Parrot Profiles: Information about your birds (name, species, dietary preferences) which you provide to customize the AI-driven experience.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Logs: We collect anonymized data on feature usage (e.g., how often the food scanner is used) to improve app performance.
  • AI Image Processing: When you use the AI Food Scanner, images are processed ephemerally. We do not store these images on our permanent servers unless you explicitly choose to save an identification result to your parrot's history.

2. Third-Party Data Processors

We partner with industry-standard providers to ensure a high-quality experience:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication, database hosting (Firestore/SQLite sync), and cloud infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat: Processes subscription metadata and ensures your premium features are synchronized across devices. No credit card data is stored by BeakBuddy.
  • Google Gemini AI: Processes text and image queries for avian care advice. Data sent to Gemini is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and we configure our API usage to prioritize data privacy.

3. AI Data Handling

BeakBuddy utilizes advanced AI models to provide safety analysis. No personal identifiers (name, email) are shared with AI providers during the analysis of food or training queries. Interaction data is used solely to provide immediate feedback to you.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we fix inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated parrot profiles at any time via the "Settings" menu in the app. This action is permanent.
  • Data Portability: Use the "Export Profile Data" feature in the app to download your parrot's history in a machine-readable format.

5. Security Measures

We use TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit and utilize Firebase's advanced security rules to ensure that only you can access your parrot's data. We perform regular security audits of our cloud infrastructure.
