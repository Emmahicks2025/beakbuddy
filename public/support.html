🦜 Parrot Care Resources

Where can I find an Avian Veterinarian?
We always recommend professional care for medical issues. Use the Association of Avian Veterinarians (AAV) Search to find a certified specialist in your area.

📱 App Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI Food Safety Scanner?
Our AI is trained on thousands of verified avian nutritional datasets. However, it is a tool for guidance, not a medical device. If the scanner gives a "Toxic" or "Caution" result, do not feed the item. If you are ever unsure, always err on the side of caution.
Can I manage multiple parrots in one account?
Yes! Use the "Switch Profile" option in the Profile tab to add and manage different parrots, each with their own training plans, diet logs, and care schedules.

🔧 Technical Troubleshooting
